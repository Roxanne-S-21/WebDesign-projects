Boats!

why i like boats

Boats, ships, submarines, and marine vessels! if its metal and floats im probably interested! there really isnt a single reason why i find boats and ships so interesting; but rather a collection of features that make marine vessels so unique and fascinating. Their history, their design, their evolution from the first canoe to a 17th century galleon to a modern warship, and their uses in entertainment, transportation and commerce all come together to make a fascinating subject of study.

My top four boats

  • Paul R Tregurtha
  • The Cutty Sark
  • Any iteration of the ship of Captain Sinbad
  • The Edmund Fitzgerald
