The crew of the Edmund Fitzgerald

The tragedy of the Edmund Fitzgerald is often overlooked but never forgotten by mariners and marine enthusiats alike; and never forgotten by the families of these sons, brothers, fathers, and good men. With a heavy heart we acknoledge the still fresh loss of these crewmembers.

Those who made shipping and sailing what it is today

This memorial is for the brave souls who braved the waters and unknown science to make ships, sailing, and the industry and culture the way it is today. Our safety regulations are written in blood, their blood. it is with a heavy and greatful heart we remember them and their work.

Those who have died servicing ships

Ship yards are kept in terrible conditions and are extremely dangerous. Corporations, in a tale as old as time, often bury these deaths under red tape and 'the risk of the industry'. This memorial is for all of the workers who have died to support this industry and the ships that are the result of it. there are far too many victims, some not even known. With a heavy heart we remember those who are the backbone of this industry.

Those lost at sea

This memorial is for all who have been lost at sea or any body of water. Captain, Officer, Deckhand, Or passenger. Known or Unknown. Military, Commercial, or Civilian. With a heavy heart we remember and mourn those lost on our waters. They are gone but not forgotten.
